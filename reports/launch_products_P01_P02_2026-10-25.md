## 3. Who each one is for (from the ICP research)
**P01** – fitted, covered-but-shapely
1. *Boot-season outfit builder:* owns tall boots, wants the dress that completes the look (strongest search evidence).
2. *Holiday-gathering dresser:* December parties/family dinners in red, green, berry or caramel (date-sensitive because of delivery).
3. *Covered-but-shapely going-out dresser.*
Avoid: wool/quality-first shoppers, petite positioning, gift buyers, maternity claims.

**P02** – relaxed, "pajamas that pass"
1. *Outfit-formula seeker:* tights + boots + this = done.
2. *Comfort-first dresser:* wants to look dressed while feeling like loungewear.
3. *Holiday-season eater:* nothing tight at the waist (time-boxed).
Avoid: tall shoppers (5'7"+) wanting a stand-alone dress; plus-size positioning (3XL garment bust is only 48 in).

## 6. Day-by-day read
- **Days 1–3 (learning):** don't judge CPA. Check that clicks arrive, that add-to-carts register in TikTok and Shopify,
  and that CTR is ≥ 1%.
- **From day 4:** compare ads on cost per add-to-cart (≤ $6.35 P01, ≤ $5.63 P02). Pause any ad that spends about one
  break-even CPA (≈ $27–30) with no purchase.
- **Winner:** 3+ purchases at or below target CPA (ROAS ≥ ~2.85) → keep and add a variation.
- **14-day budget ≈ $700:** ≈ 25 purchases break even, ≈ 35 hit target.
