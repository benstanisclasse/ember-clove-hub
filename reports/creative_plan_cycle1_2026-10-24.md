## Production and review steps
1. Build the two photo-motion bases and four hook versions (free). Tool: TikTok Ads Manager video editor, CapCut, or locally with ffmpeg (needs owner OK to install).
2. Make **one** AI try-on base first and check it against the real dress (cable pattern, slit, neckline, colors). Reject anything that changes the garment.
3. Make the second AI base, then the hook versions.
4. Record each in `recipes/` and `data/creatives.csv` (recipe.py); mark AI ads "AI-generated" in TikTok.
5. Owner reviews all 8 before anything is uploaded.
