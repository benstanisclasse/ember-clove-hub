Check before starting

  • Follower minimum. TikTok's US affiliate program has required about 5,000 followers to join. No commission is earned before then. This rule isn't verified for 2026.
  • AI try-on rules. An AI model "wearing" another seller's product that you've never seen is a misleading-content risk. TikTok Shop has rules on this.
  • Separate account. Promoting other shops' products from @emberandclove would blur the brand.
  • Your rules still apply: the presenter is labelled as AI, and there are no "I bought it" claims.

About the data

  • The 5 dresses were chosen because they sell, so average products will do worse.
  • The AI odds count 157 selling AI videos out of an estimated 1,353 AI videos. The zero-sales videos Kalodata didn't list were assumed to be AI in the same share (45%) as the listed ones.
  • Reach scales each video's sales. In reality, low reach also makes a hit less likely.
  • Kalodata sales are estimates. Source: research/kalodata/video_sales_distribution_2026-10-25.json.
