# Product naming & copy proposal — 2026-10-24 (NOT APPLIED)

Store has 53 active products: 40 EPROLO (20 apparel + 20 sandals) and 13 old wellness/POD items.

## Naming rules (site-wide)
1. **Format:** `[Key detail] [Fabric/feature] [Item]` — 3–5 words, Title Case. E.g. "Mock Neck Knit Midi Dress".
2. **Lead with what a shopper searches** (item type, length, neckline, fabric). Good for Google Shopping and TikTok search.
3. **Remove:** "Women's" (the whole store is women's), "Sexy", "Elegant", "Luxury", "Designer", "Slimming", "Eco-Friendly", "Cross Border", "Fashion", supplier jargon, duplicate words.
4. **No unverifiable claims:** no "eco", "designer", "genuine leather", "handmade". PU = "faux leather".
5. **Unique names:** no two products share a name.
6. **Colors:** warm, clear names: "Grandma Gray" → "Heather Gray", "Purplish Red" → "Berry", "Color" → fix per photo.
7. **Shoe sizes:** show US sizes: "36(US5)" → "US 5". EU-only sizes converted.

## Description template (every product)
> **[One warm sentence on how it feels/wears.]**
>
> **Details**
> - Fabric: …
> - Fit: … (true to size / relaxed / runs small)
> - Length / heel height: …
>
> **Size & fit** — size chart in **inches**.
>
> **Care** — …
>
> **Shipping** — Ships in X–Y business days with tracking. Free US shipping over $70.

Words like "sexy", "slimming" and style-tag lists are removed. No health or body-shaping claims.
