## Positioning
- **Who:** women ~25–45 who want comfortable pieces that still look put-together.
- **What:** cozy knitwear, sweaters, dresses and skirts in warm, earthy tones.
- **Feel:** warm, soft, effortless. Never loud, never "sexy".
- **Name story:** Ember = warmth that lingers; Clove = the warm spice palette (coffee, caramel, oat, burgundy) the collection is built around.
- **Avoid (not true for this supply chain):** "handcrafted", "small-batch", "ethically made", "sustainable", fake urgency, invented "regular prices".

## Product title formula
`[Feel/detail] [Item] – [Key feature]` — e.g.
- "Grassland turkey pattern printed round neck fleece hoodie" → **Cozy Printed Fleece Sweatshirt – Relaxed Fit**
- "Elegant Contrast Stripe Knit Cardigan Women's Top with Lapel Collar Slimming Fit" → **Contrast Collar Knit Cardigan**
- "Half high collar knee length slim fit woolen dress" → **Mock Neck Knit Midi Dress**

Plus for every product: remove "Sexy" and supplier style tags, convert size charts to inches, set category, product type, gender and age group.
