# Customer Acquisition, Conversion and Retention Benchmarks (US Shopify DTC: cat-home base case and De'Longhi Dedica espresso-kit shadow case), as of Oct 19, 2026

Conventions used in these notes:
- **[VENDOR]** = the figure comes from a platform, agency or tool vendor using its own customer sample. It is not audited and is not independent.
- **[SECONDHAND]** = I saw the figure only in an aggregator or search summary that attributes it to a primary source. I could not open the primary source. Triple Whale's own pages returned HTTP 403 to my fetches.
- **[FILING]** = SEC filing, opened directly.
- **[ASSUMPTION]** = planning assumption I propose because no adequate source exists. Each one has pessimistic, base and optimistic values. These are not sourced facts.
- **[STALE]** = data older than about 12 months, or a dataset window that ends before Q4 2025.
- Most "2026 benchmark" pages in search results are SEO content from agencies or AI tools (eightx.co, eevy.ai, adriselab, coreppc, webtonic and others). Their numbers often conflict and their methods are not stated. I mark them low-confidence and prefer filings and named vendor datasets.

## Q1. Paid social benchmarks (Meta and TikTok) for pet and home/kitchen, seasonality, and new stores versus established brands

### Takeaway
The best-sourced 2026 Meta medians come from Triple Whale's dataset of 40,000+ ecommerce brands (Aug 2025 to Jul 2026). For Pets & Animals: CPM $16.27, CTR 2.37%, CPA $38.51, ROAS 1.60. For Home & Garden: CPM $14.67, CTR 2.38%, CPA $47.93, ROAS 2.25. These are medians for established brands with trained pixels, blended across prospecting and retargeting. A new store with no pixel history should plan for worse CPA. Meta auction prices are rising: average price per ad was +9% in 2025 and +12% year on year in each of Q1 and Q2 2026. Q4 CPMs climb through late November.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Implied AOV check: CPA × ROAS gives about $61.6 for Pets ($38.51 × 1.60) and about $108 for Home & Garden ($47.93 × 2.25). The pet figure sits close to a $39–69 fountain first order. On those medians, a pet brand's platform-attributed first-order revenue is only about 1.6x spend. After COGS, shipping and fees, first-order contribution is likely negative or near breakeven at the median.
- The Triple Whale CTR of about 2.4% is probably an all-clicks CTR. Link CTR for conversion campaigns is typically lower. That makes the WordStream 1.85% traffic-campaign CTR and $0.51 CPC optimistic for a sales-objective campaign.
- **[ASSUMPTION] New store with no pixel data, Meta prospecting, non-Q4, cat fountain:**

  | Metric | Pessimistic | Base | Optimistic |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | CPM | $24 | $18 | $14 |
  | Link CTR | 0.8% | 1.1% | 1.5% |
  | CPC (link) | $3.00 | $1.64 | $0.93 |
  | Landing CVR | 0.8% | 1.3% | 2.0% |
  | First-order CPA | ~$375 (see note) | ~$126 | ~$47 |

  Note on the pessimistic CPA: compounding all the pessimistic inputs gives about $375, which is not realistic because a brand would stop spending first. Cap the modeled CPA instead:
  - **First-order paid CPA for months 1–3:** $90 / $60 / $40.
  - **Months 4–12, after creative and pixel learning:** $65 / $45 / $32.

  The base case sits roughly 15% above Triple Whale's $38.51 pet median plus a new-store penalty.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Q4 seasonality multiplier on CPM:**
  - **Late Oct to mid Nov:** ×1.10 / 1.10 / 1.05.
  - **BFCM week:** ×1.50 / 1.30 / 1.15.
  - **December to the 20th:** ×1.30 / 1.20 / 1.10.
  - **January:** ×0.85 / 0.85 / 0.80.
  - Grounding: GeistM shows cumulative CPM rises of about 20%+ from late October through November, with some verticals at +36–42%. Cats and pet care may see a CVR lift at BFCM that partly offsets the CPM rise.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Year-over-year CPM drift:** +8% / +10% / +5% a year. This is anchored on Meta's +9% to +12% price per ad.
- **[ASSUMPTION] TikTok as a secondary channel:** CPM $6 / $4.50 / $3.50, CVR 0.6% / 1.0% / 1.5%, CPA $70 / $45 / $28. TikTok in-app attribution undercounts sales, so model it on blended MER (total revenue ÷ total ad spend), not on platform ROAS.
- **Shadow case (espresso kit, $59–99):**
  - Use Home & Garden as the anchor: CPA about $48 and ROAS about 2.25 at a median AOV of about $108.
  - Targeting is narrow (Dedica owners only), which tends to raise CPM and cap scale.
  - **[ASSUMPTION]** CPA $85 / $60 / $40.

### Gaps
- I could not open Triple Whale's primary benchmark pages (HTTP 403). Category sample sizes and exact metric definitions are not verified.
- I found no Varos, Common Thread, Motion, Lebesgue or Statista pet-specific 2026 tables. Motion publishes creative benchmarks, not CPA by vertical.
- I found no published dataset that separates new-store (no pixel) CPA from that of established brands. The new-store penalty above is an assumption.
- I found no platform-wide 2025 BFCM CPM figure from Meta or from a large aggregator.

## Q2. Conversion rates by category and traffic source, pet AOV, and single-product landing pages

### Takeaway
There is no authoritative category CVR. Vendor and blog sources put Shopify pet-supplies median CVR at about 1.8–2.3% and kitchen/home appliances at about 2.8–3.0%, against a Shopify-wide blended rate of about 1.4%. Email traffic converts at roughly 3–4x the rate of paid social (about 4.2% versus about 1.1%, figures attributed to Littledata). Pet AOV benchmarks cluster around $45–85.

## Q3. Pet subscriptions (Chewy, attach rates, churn, consumer willingness) and coffee subscriptions

### Takeaway
Pet consumables subscriptions are strong at scale: Chewy's Autoship customers generated 84.6% of net sales in Q2 FY2026. Credible monthly churn for pet replenishment sits roughly between 3.4% (Recharge pet figure, secondhand) and 6.3% (Subbly replenishment median). The one hard attach-type datapoint is VetRxDirect: 34% of subscribers also buy add-ons, and subscriptions are 20% of its revenue. Coffee subscription churn is higher and more variable: about 5–10% a month typically, with a best-in-class roaster at 2–3%.

### Cited Findings
**Chewy (largest pet autoship signal)**
- 13 weeks ended Aug 2, 2026 versus Aug 3, 2025 [FILING] — [Chewy 10-Q, Q2 FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001766502/000162828026061018/chwy-20260802.htm):
  - Autoship customer sales: $2,817.2M (+9.3%), equal to 84.6% of net sales (83.0% a year earlier).
  - Active customers: 21.705M (+3.8%).
  - Net sales per active customer: $602 (+1.9%).
  - Advertising and marketing: $214.8M, which I calculate at about 6.4% of $3,330.2M net sales.
  - Gross margin: about 30.4% (calculated).
- Fiscal 2025: Autoship was 83.3% of net sales, up from 79.2% the year before. — [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/blog/chewy-autoship-customers-drive-84-6-of-net-sales)
- Definition caveat: Chewy's "Autoship customer sales" includes **all** purchases by customers who had an Autoship order ship in the prior 364 days, not only scheduled deliveries. It overstates the share of revenue that is literally on subscription. — [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/blog/chewy-autoship-customers-drive-84-6-of-net-sales)

**Recharge (pet vertical)**
- Recharge's pet page (undated, apparently 2026) claims [VENDOR] — [Recharge, pet vertical page](https://getrecharge.com/verticals/pets/):
  - 1,500+ pet brands, 8M pet subscribers and $4B GMV.
  - Average LTV of $429, with the basis undefined.
  - Case-study headlines:
    - Open Farm reduced churn by nearly 29% with Cancellation Prevention.
    - Wild Earth recovered 88% of failed transactions.
    - Fuzzball raised subscriber LTV 90%.
- Pet subscription monthly churn of 3.4% (2.5% voluntary plus 0.9% involuntary), attributed to Recharge's 2026 analysis. [SECONDHAND, unverified] — [Foundry CRO](https://foundrycro.com/blog/dtc-pet-marketing-benchmarks-2026/)
- Recharge's broader DTC panel: 7.1% monthly churn (4.1% voluntary plus 3.0% involuntary). [SECONDHAND] — [eightx churn index](https://eightx.co/blog/dtc-subscription-churn-index-2026)

**Other churn benchmarks**
- Subbly platform data (Sep–Nov 2024, published Jan 2025): DTC replenishment median monthly churn of 6.31%; curation-box median of 7.1%. [VENDOR] [STALE] — [Subbly churn report](https://www.subbly.co/blog/subscription-churn-data-report/), cited via [eightx pet churn](https://eightx.co/blog/pet-subscription-churn-rate-benchmark)

The article itself says its 12-month retention bands and its "about 44% of cancellations happen in the first 90 days" figure are "working ranges", not data. [Low confidence] — [eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/pet-subscription-churn-rate-benchmark)

**Attach and subscription share (pet)**

  - 34% of subscribers buy additional one-off items.

  - 3,000 active subscribers in the program's first year.
  - Offer: 5% off the first order and 10% off Auto-Delivery orders.

**Consumer willingness**
- I found no 2025–2026 APPA survey statistic on autoship or subscription use (the full report is paywalled).
- The latest datapoint found is from 2019: 12% of dog owners bought treats via online subscription or autoship. [STALE] — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1086599/pet-owners-using-online-treat-subscriptions-us)

**Coffee (shadow case)**
- Peace Coffee on Loop [VENDOR] — [Loop case study](https://www.loopwork.co/customer-stories/peace-coffee):
  - Monthly churn of 2–3%, measured February 2026.
  - Month-1 cohort retention of 84.8% (January 2026 cohort).
  - Cancellation-flow save rate rose from about 16% to about 25%.
  - Acquisition offer restructured from 40% off one order to 26% off each of the first three orders.
- Brûlerie Camélia: monthly churn of 9.2% before its retention program and 6.0% after. [VENDOR] — [Fullmetrix](https://fullmetrix.com/en/case-studies/abonnement-cafe-retention)
- Subscribfy (Shopify merchant data, May 5, 2026): 6-month retention of 74% on a 30-day default cadence versus 41% on a 60-day cadence. [VENDOR] — [Subscribfy](https://hub.subscribfy.com/docs/blog/2026-05-05-what-drives-retention-in-coffee-subscriptions-on-shopify)
- Typical coffee churn of 5–10% a month (healthy programs 4–7%), and about 28% cancel within 90 days. [Low confidence] — [eightx coffee](https://eightx.co/blog/coffee-subscription-churn-rate-benchmark)
- Average coffee churn of 11% a month, citing Moriondo/CodingKart. [Low confidence] — [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/coffee-subscription-customer-service)
- I found no Trade, Atlas, Bean Box or Dripshipper disclosures of churn or attach rates.

### Inferences
- A 90-day filter pack has a lower per-month cost and fewer "decision points" than a monthly plan. Per-shipment churn should be modeled per 90-day cycle, not per month. A 4–7% monthly equivalent maps to roughly 12–20% per quarterly shipment (1 − (1 − m)^3).
- Fountain filters are a functional consumable: the product degrades without them. That is closer to "vet/pharmacy replenishment" than to treats, which supports a churn rate at or below the pet-food medians. Many buyers will, however, buy generic third-party filters on Amazon, which raises the risk of leakage.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Subscription attach rate** (share of new fountain buyers who start a filter subscription at checkout or within 30 days): 10% / 20% / 30%.
  - Grounding is limited: VetRxDirect's recurring revenue is 20% of total, and Chewy's autoship base is very high but only at mature scale.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Filter subscription per-90-day-cycle churn:**
  - **First renewal:** 35% / 25% / 15%.
  - **Later cycles:** 20% / 14% / 9%.
  - These are about 7% / 5% / 3% monthly equivalents, bracketing Subbly's 6.31% and Recharge's secondhand 3.4%.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Involuntary churn (failed payments) after dunning:** 1.0% / 0.7% / 0.4% a month equivalent. Recharge's secondhand split puts involuntary churn at 0.9%.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Coffee bean subscription (shadow case):**
  - **Attach rate on kit buyers:** 8% / 15% / 25%.
  - **Monthly churn:** 10% / 7% / 4%.
  - **First-month drop:** 25% / 18% / 12%.
  - Default to a 30-day cadence, per Subscribfy's retention gap.

### Gaps
- I could not open the Recharge 2026 Subscription Trend Report, so pet churn and attach figures from it remain unverified.
- No Skio, Subscribe Pro or Ordergroove pet churn benchmark was found.
- No public case study on pet-fountain filter subscriptions (PETLIBRO, Catit, Veken, uahpet) was found.
- No current (2025–2026) US consumer survey on pet subscription willingness was found.

### Takeaway
Hard pet-specific repeat-rate benchmarks are scarce. The cross-category ecommerce average repeat purchase rate is about 28% (vendor). Klaviyo's 2026 benchmarks show flows producing about 41% of email revenue from 5.3% of sends. Klaviyo's email share of total store revenue is reported secondhand at about 27%, from a cohort that skews toward mature brands. Klaviyo publishes no pet category in its readable benchmark text.

### Cited Findings
- Klaviyo 2026 benchmarks (183,000+ customers; publication date not stated) [VENDOR] — [Klaviyo email benchmarks by industry](https://www.klaviyo.com/marketing-resources/email-benchmarks-by-industry); [Klaviyo benchmarks](https://klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks):

## Q5. Refunds, returns and chargebacks

### Takeaway
US online return rates average about 19% across categories (NRF 2025 projection). Pet supplies sit far lower: 1.2% in Swiss retail data and about 8% in a Statista global list. Household appliances are about 8% on the same Statista list. Ecommerce chargeback rates average roughly 0.2–0.6%. ## Q7. Payback and CAC norms, and public pet-company acquisition data

### Takeaway
Neither Chewy nor BARK discloses CAC.
- **Chewy:** spends about 6.4% of net sales on advertising and marketing (Q2 FY2026), with $602 net sales per active customer and 84.6% of sales from Autoship customers.
- **BARK:** cut advertising and marketing 29% to $59.2M (15.0% of revenue) in FY2026 while total orders fell 23.8%. This illustrates the volume cost of pulling back acquisition spend.

Operator and investor payback norms circulating in 2026 (blog-sourced, low confidence):
- A median DTC CAC payback of about 3.4 months.
- 6–12 months for pure DTC.
- 3–9 months for subscription brands.
- Under 12 months on a contribution-margin basis as an investor floor.

### Cited Findings
- Chewy Q2 FY2026 [FILING] — [Chewy 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001766502/000162828026061018/chwy-20260802.htm):
  - Advertising and marketing: $214.8M (+7.1%), about 6.4% of net sales (calculated).
  - Active customers: 21.705M (+3.8%).
  - Net sales per active customer: $602.
  - CAC is not disclosed.
- BARK FY2026 (fiscal year ended Mar 31, 2026) [FILING] — [BARK 10-K FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001819574/000162828026042242/bark-20260331.htm):
  - Total orders of 10.06M, down 23.8% from 13.21M.
  - AOV of $31.06.
  - DTC revenue of $324.9M, down 21.9%.
  - DTC gross margin of 68.4% in the KPI table (excluding BARK Air) and 65.8% in the MD&A text.
  - Advertising and marketing of $59.2M (15.0% of revenue), down from $83.8M (17.3%).
  - The filing describes "a strategic decrease in DTC marketing spend".
  - No subscriber count, churn or CAC figure in the 10-K text I read.
- BARK management, per a secondary earnings summary: Q3 FY2026 was the "lowest customer acquisition cost quarter in nearly three years". This is a characterization, not a number. [SECONDHAND] — [Panabee](https://www.panabee.com/news/bark-earnings-q3-2026)
- Payback norms [low confidence; methods not stated] — [eightx CAC payback by vertical](https://eightx.co/blog/average-cac-payback-period-by-vertical); [eightx payback benchmark](https://eightx.co/blog/cac-payback-period-benchmark):
  - Median DTC payback of about 3.4 months, with a target under 4 months.
  - $3–10M brands typically run 6–10 months.
  - Subscription brands run 3–9 months and pure DTC 6–12 months.
  - Food & beverage runs 1–3 months.
  - Investors want payback under 12 months measured on contribution margin.
- I found no Common Thread or Varos published payback standard in search.
- A commonly cited operating ladder: payback under 3 months is excellent, 3–6 good, 6–12 acceptable, and over 12 a red flag. [Agency blog] — [ATTN Agency](https://www.attnagency.com/blog/cac-payback-period)

### Inferences
- Chewy's 6.4% advertising-to-sales ratio reflects a mature, autoship-heavy base. BARK's 15–17% is a better reference for a DTC brand spending on paid acquisition. A launch-stage single-product brand will run far higher; 25–40% of revenue in year 1 is common practice, but that range is an [ASSUMPTION], not sourced.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Model thresholds:**
  - **First-order contribution after paid CAC** (gross margin minus shipping, fees and CAC): at least -$10 / at least $0 / at least +$8 per new customer. "$0" means first-order breakeven, the common bootstrapped-DTC rule.
  - **Contribution-margin CAC payback:** 9 / 4 / 2 months. Mark payback beyond 12 months as non-investable.
  - **12-month LTV:CAC on contribution margin:** 1.2x / 1.8x / 2.5x. The base case requires the filter subscription to carry the brand past 1.5x.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Blended CAC, all channels** (including organic, affiliate and email-referred first orders): paid CAC × 0.95 / × 0.85 / × 0.75 in year 1, improving to × 0.85 / × 0.75 / × 0.65 in year 2 as organic and referral traffic builds.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Illustrative base-case check for the fountain, using the assumptions above:**

  | Item | Value |
  |---|---|
  | AOV | $62 |
  | Gross margin before shipping | about 60% (assumed) |
  | Shipping, fees and pick/pack | about $12 |
  | First-order contribution before CAC | about $25 |
  | Paid CAC (months 4–12) | $45 |
  | First-order contribution after CAC | about -$20 |

  The gap is recovered by a 20% subscription attach and 20% non-subscriber repeat buying. Filter packs at about $20 with about 65% margin earn about $13 contribution per shipment after shipping, so payback runs roughly 6–9 months in the base case. This is an inference from my own assumptions, not a benchmark. The cost and margin inputs should come from the COGS research.
