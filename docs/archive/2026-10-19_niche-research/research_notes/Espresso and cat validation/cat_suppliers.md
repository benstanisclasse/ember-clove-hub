# Cat Home Category: Supplier and Product Feasibility (Phase D + D1), US, as of 19 Oct 2026

Method and access notes, for the report writer:
- AliExpress (aliexpress.us) listings, prices, sold counts and review data were read live on **19 Oct 2026** in a real browser session with a US ship-to. Review statistics come from AliExpress's own feedback endpoint (`feedback.aliexpress.com/pc/searchEvaluation.do?productId=...`). Prices are the displayed price for the default or lowest variant, and many include a "New shoppers save" promotion, so the AutoDS buy price may be higher. Treat every price as a snapshot that will drift.
- **CJdropshipping was blocked.** Every product page redirected to a "Human verification" (CAPTCHA) page at frontend.cjdropshipping.com, and I did not attempt to bypass it. CJ product pages are also barely indexed by search. Only one CJ fountain URL was found, and its price came from a search snippet.
- **Alibaba**: one search-results page loaded (19 Oct 2026). Every product-detail page and later search after that hit a CAPTCHA. So customization MOQs, sample costs and lead times per Alibaba listing were **not** readable, and only the card-level price, MOQ, supplier, years and rating were captured.
- **1688 was blocked**: it redirected to a Taobao/1688 login wall. No 1688 data.
- **Global Sources** returned HTTP 429. Its data comes from search snippets.
- **Spocket and Zendrop** catalogs need a login. Only public pages and snippets were used.
- The CPSC recall database was checked directly by downloading CPSC's full recall CSV (https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/recall-data/recalls_recall_listing.csv; 10,099 records, latest dated 15 Oct 2026) and filtering it.

## Q1. Representative product listings (AliExpress / CJ / Alibaba / US-warehouse), with readiness ratings

### Takeaway
Plenty of high-volume, well-rated AliExpress listings exist for the stainless fountain, cordless fountain, generic filters and litter mats, at $17–39 per fountain, $0.36–1.39 per filter and $1–7 per mat, with free shipping and 6–12 day US delivery. **None of them shows UL/ETL, FCC or UN38.3 evidence on the listing**, and review data is heavily positive-skewed (AliExpress), so pump and battery durability can't be verified from listings. Scratchers with replaceable inserts are thin on AliExpress: plenty of basic cardboard pads, but few true "frame + refill" systems.

### Inferences
- AliExpress review data looks strongly positive-skewed: 0 one-star reviews on most fountain listings, which is implausible against Western retail patterns of pump failure at 1–12 months. Use Amazon/Chewy reviews of the same OEM designs, not AliExpress stars, to judge durability.
- The same OEM design is sold by many stores at very different prices ($21.81 snippet vs $37.92 live; $34.08 vs $38.93 vs $44.72 for the "3L wireless" clone). This allows supplier switching but also shows how prices swing.
- "Ships from United States" AliExpress stock exists but at low volume (13–112 sold for basic stainless models), so it's thin as a primary source.

### Gaps
- CJdropshipping (including the CJ US warehouse), Spocket and Zendrop unit cost, shipping, warehouse and inventory were not retrievable (CAPTCHA or login). AutoDS "private supplier" catalogs need a login.
- No listing showed UL/ETL adapter marks, FCC IDs or UN38.3 test summaries. They would have to be requested from sellers.
- Packaging specs (box dimensions, drop-test) are not stated on listings, beyond reviewer reports of dents.
- Large-size variant prices for mats were not captured.

## Q4. Landed cost per hero SKU: AutoDS test vs private label (ALL ESTIMATES LABELED)

### Takeaway
**Tariff risk for the fountain is higher than the ~24% blended China rate from Checkpoint 2.** CBP classifies electric pet fountains under **HTS 8413.70.2004 (submersible pumps), MFN Free, plus Section 301 List 1 at 25% (9903.88.01)**. Stacking the **new 12.5% Section 301 forced-labor duty (24 Jul 2026)** gives an **estimated 37.5% on China-origin fountains**. Estimated landed cost: about **$31–51 per corded fountain at the AutoDS stage** versus about **$18–27 delivered at the private-label/3PL stage**.

### Cited Findings
- CBP NY N303286 (18 Mar 2019): a "Stream Pet Fountain" from China (plastic bowl, submersible pump, charcoal filters) is classified **8413.70.2004, general duty Free**, with an additional **25%** Section 301 duty (report 9903.88.01). NY N300736 reached the same classification. CBP rulings say fountains with submersible pumps are "more specifically provided for in subheading 8413.70.2004". — [CustomsMobile N303286](https://www.customsmobile.com/rulings/docview?doc_id=NY+N303286&highlight=3502*); [N300736](https://www.customsmobile.com/rulings/docview?doc_id=NY+N300736&highlight=NY+N300736); [CBP N235013](https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/N235013)

## Q5. D1: Subscription filter fulfillment verification

### Takeaway
The flow technically works: Appstle or Seal creates a recurring Shopify order, AutoDS imports it like any order and auto-buys from the supplier. But China-sourced filters are a weak base for 2–4 week renewal promises. Delivery is 6–12 days (with documented customs holds of about 2 weeks), prices drift (one clone listing at $37.92 vs a $21.81 snippet), stock caps and removals happen ("Max 1 pcs/shopper", "Only 6 left", a "no longer available" listing), and AutoDS only auto-processes orders after FBA is configured, with manual resends for older failures. **Stock filters in a US 3PL from about the first 100–200 active subscribers (estimate)**, or sooner, since filters are tiny and cheap to hold.

### Cited Findings
- AutoDS FBA: places orders on the supplier using AutoDS's buyer account after checking wallet balance and Auto-Order Credits. "Only orders received after you save the FBA settings are processed automatically". Insufficient-funds orders resend automatically only if placed within the last 24 hours; older ones need manual resend. Orders can't be cancelled once "Ordered". Tracking syncs to the channel about 24–48 h after the order reaches "Ordered". There is no automatic wallet recharge. — [AutoDS help](https://help.autods.com/en/articles/12700443-automate-your-orders-with-fulfilled-by-autods-fba)
- A search summary of AutoDS docs indicates orders older than 30 days are auto-rejected, and that subscription services are not a supported product type. I could not confirm this on the fetched portion of the page. — [AutoDS help (snippet)](https://help.autods.com/en/articles/12700443-automate-your-orders-with-fulfilled-by-autods-fba)
- No documentation was found of a native AutoDS integration with Appstle or Seal. Subscription renewals arrive in AutoDS as ordinary Shopify orders. — [Appstle app](https://apps.shopify.com/subscriptions-by-appstle); [Seal manual](https://www.sealsubscriptions.com/articles/manual)

### Inferences
- **Recommended design (inference)**: ship a 3- or 6-month filter pack (e.g. 6 or 12 filters) every 90 days rather than a 2–4 week drip. That decouples customer experience from China transit and cuts per-shipment cost. If testing on AutoDS, set the renewal date about 3 weeks before the customer needs filters.
- **When to move filters to a 3PL (estimate)**: once monthly filter shipments are high enough that a $500/month 3PL minimum is roughly matched by pick/pack activity. At about $3.25 per order plus storage, that is about 100–150 shipments/month (≈ 300–450 subscribers on a quarterly cadence, or 100–150 on monthly). Start earlier if using a no-minimum pet 3PL. A 500–1,600-unit filter buy at $0.45–0.59 costs only about $250–950, so inventory risk is small.
- Failure handling to build in: Appstle retries (default 8 tries, 1 day apart), then **skip** (not cancel) to preserve the subscriber. Use tagging so AutoDS or the 3PL doesn't fulfill unpaid orders. Tracking sync from AutoDS takes 24–48 h after purchase, and the 3PL syncs directly to Shopify.
- Why AutoDS/China is weak for 4–8 week renewals: (1) 6–12+ day transit plus customs variance; (2) listing price drift on the same SKU; (3) per-shopper caps and stock-outs; (4) listing removal ("no longer available"); (5) filter-fit changes when a seller swaps OEM; (6) no branded packaging, so subscribers see generic AliExpress parcels; (7) AutoDS wallet and credit failures stall orders silently.

### Gaps
- Unconfirmed: does Seal or Appstle expose a renewal-order tag that AutoDS can filter on? No documentation found.
- The AutoDS 30-day rejection rule and the "subscription products unsupported" statement came from a search summary only.

## Q6. Safety / regulatory

### Takeaway
**No CPSC recall of a pet water fountain from 2019 through 15 Oct 2026** was found in CPSC's full recall CSV. Pet-category recalls in 2023–2026 were for button-cell batteries in pet toys and bacterial contamination in liquid pet sprays and shampoos. The compliance priorities for a private-label fountain:
- a UL 1310 Class 2 (or ETL-listed equivalent) adapter;
- FCC Part 15 Subpart B SDoC for any digital or sensor electronics, with a US responsible party;
- a UN 38.3 test summary for cordless lithium models;
- material and Prop 65 diligence (BPA notices are common, and a 2026 notice targeted lead in pet bowls).

### Cited Findings
- **CPSC recalls**: filtering CPSC's recall CSV (10,099 records, through 15 Oct 2026) for "fountain" (excluding fireworks) from 2019 onward returned **zero pet-fountain recalls**. 2023–2026 pet-related recalls include: JC Sales Lil' Buddies Pet Laser Toys (Apr 2026, 51,160 units, button-cell battery access); Petmate Pet Zone toys (Aug 2025, 15,400 units, button-cell); CURiO Capri Blue pet sprays/shampoos (Oct 2023, about 59,000, *Pseudomonas*); Spectrum Brands FURminator conditioner (Jun 2024, about 102,000, *Pseudomonas*); Bausch + Lomb Project Watson dog eyelid wipes (May 2024, about 32,000, bacteria/fungi). Also notable: the OCOOPA rechargeable hand warmer recall (Jul 2026, 1.5 million units, lithium-ion fire, one death). — [CPSC recall CSV](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/recall-data/recalls_recall_listing.csv)

Aftermarket fountain adapters on Amazon advertise UL/Class 2 but are unverified seller claims. — [Amazon UpBright PetSafe adapter](https://www.amazon.com/PetSafe-Drinkwell-360-Power-Supply/dp/B0BB7G1H8K); [UL-listed 5V Petlibro adapter](https://www.amazon.com/Adapter-USBC-PETLIBRO-PLWF106-Replacement/dp/B0GF6K8BR4)
- **FCC**: under 47 CFR 15.101, unintentional radiators must be authorized before marketing, by certification or SDoC. The SDoC route covers most non-radio digital devices, Class B applies to home use, and an SDoC needs a **US-based responsible party**. — [eCFR Part 15 Subpart B](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-15/subpart-B); [ComplianceGate SDoC guide](https://compliancegate.com/?p=7230). Petlibro's Dockstream 2 cordless fountain (PLWF116S) carries **FCC ID 2A3DE-PLWF116S**, i.e. it has a radio and is certified rather than SDoC. — [Dockstream 2 manual](https://device.report/manual/19838437)
- **Lithium**: lithium cells and batteries must be of a type that passed UN 38.3 to be transported. Test summaries remain valid unless the design changes (PHMSA interpretation 23-0040). — [PHMSA interpretation](https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/sites/phmsa.dot.gov/files/docs/standards-rulemaking/hazmat/interpretations/77931/230040.pdf); [ComplianceGate UN38.3](https://www.compliancegate.com/un-38-3-guide/). Checkpoint 2 noted that Amazon requires UN 38.3 for lithium products.
- **Prop 65 / BPA**: Prop 65 60-day notices alleging unwarned BPA exposure are common, and each unwarned sale can be alleged as a separate violation. A 2026 notice alleged lead exposure from pet bowls. No pet-fountain-specific notice was found. — [OAG notice 2026-04105](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/prop65/notices/2026-04105.pdf); [OAG notice 2020-01324](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/prop65/notices/2020-01324.pdf); [Intertek Prop 65 analysis Oct 2023](https://intertek.com/products-retail/insight-bulletins/2023/prop-65-60-day-notices-analysis-10-2023)
- **FIFRA reminder (from Checkpoint 2)**: "absorbs/traps odor" is acceptable; "kills bacteria" makes the product a pesticide claim. This applies to deodorizer copy and to any "antibacterial" filter or fountain claims. Note that some China deodorizer listings use "sterilization/antibacterial" language. — [ship4wd deodorant listing](https://sourcing-sb.ship4wd.com/product/pet-deodorant-cat-and-dog-deodorant-spray-cat-litter-cat-nest-urine-odor-removal-indoor-sterilization-pet-household-deodorant)

### Inferences
- Since there is no recall history for pet fountains, the category's main safety exposure is the **adapter** (shock or fire) and, for cordless units, the **lithium cell**. Both are controllable in private label by specifying a UL 1310-listed adapter with its UL file number on the label, and demanding a UN 38.3 summary plus an IEC 62133 report. For the AutoDS test stage, prefer USB-powered SKUs and tell customers to use a certified USB adapter, or source US-listed adapters separately.
- Food-contact: use 304 stainless for the water path, and request LFGB/FDA-style food-contact test reports for plastics (BPA-free). Avoid "antibacterial" or "sterilizing" claims.

### Gaps
- No UL Product iQ lookup was performed (it needs a file number from the supplier).
- No pet-fountain-specific Prop 65 notice found. The OAG database was not exhaustively searched.
- FDA does not regulate pet water fountains as such. I didn't verify any state-level pet product registration needs for litter deodorizers (some states register "pet products", or pesticides if claims are made).
