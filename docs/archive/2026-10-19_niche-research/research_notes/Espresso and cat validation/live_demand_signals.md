# Live Demand Signals: Home Espresso Accessories (Breville/De'Longhi) vs Cat Water Fountains / Cat-Home (US)

Collection method: Built-in browser pane (mcp__Claude_Browser__*), viewed 2026-10-19, roughly 23:00–23:52 UTC. Google Trends data was taken from Trends' own `widgetdata/multiline` and `relatedsearches` JSON endpoints, which the Explore page calls itself, by running page JS on trends.google.com. This gave exact index values rather than chart readings. Amazon and AliExpress values were read from page DOM text. Nobody was signed in anywhere. No forms were submitted except search URLs. No CAPTCHAs came up.

Sites I could not reach: facebook.com (Meta Ad Library), where navigation was "denied or failed" by the browser pane, and old.reddit.com, which is "not allowed due to safety restrictions". WebFetch fallback to reddit.com was also refused. TikTok Creative Center opened, but almost everything was behind a login wall.

## Q1. Google Trends (US): 5-year and 90-day interest

### Takeaway
Cat water fountain search interest is clearly growing. After a site-wide anomaly in spring 2026 (explained below), Aug–Oct 2026 interest is about +36% to +67% year over year, well above control terms that rose about +5–15%. "Espresso machine" is growing modestly, about +12–25% YoY in Aug–Oct, only slightly ahead of the controls. Espresso accessory terms grew a lot from 2021 to 2025, but they are tiny in absolute terms (under 1 on a scale where "espresso machine" is 100). In Aug–Oct 2026 they were mostly flat to slightly up YoY. They are strongly seasonal, roughly doubling to 2.6x in Nov–Dec.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Cat water fountain** shows the strongest live demand signal. Interest rose steadily from 2021 to 2025 (+44% from 2022 to 2025), and after the spring-2026 anomaly it is +36–67% YoY. That is several times the control drift. New brands are appearing in rising queries (Selene, Pawira, Kitty Spout, Bissell), which points to an active, crowding market.
- **Espresso machine** demand is growing, at roughly +12–25% YoY in Aug–Oct. That is only modestly above the controls, so it reads as "Growing, mildly", consistent with the NCA's 40%→45% past-week espresso figure. Breville dominates the related queries, and new budget brands are breaking out (Casabrews, Chefman, Thyme & Table, Meraki), which widens the base of ~51/54mm accessory buyers.
- **Espresso accessory** terms grew 2–4x from 2022 to 2025 but stayed tiny in absolute terms. Since summer 2026 they have been roughly flat YoY, apart from puck screen (+11–30%). They are highly seasonal, at 2–2.6x in December. "Normcore" breaks out in every accessory query, meaning one brand dominates branded accessory searches.
- Inferred absolute size: "cat water fountain" search volume appears to be several times that of any single espresso accessory term. In the 12-month comparison, cat water fountain sits at 3–4 while puck screen and bottomless portafilter sit at 0–1.

### Gaps
- Google Trends gives relative indices, not absolute search volume. I could not get absolute keyword volumes because Keyword Planner requires login.
- The spring-2026 anomaly makes 2026 calendar averages and 5-year peak dates unreliable. I could not confirm its cause.
- I did not take Trends screenshots of the charts. The numbers come straight from the API that feeds the chart.
- I did not test De'Longhi-specific terms or litter-mat terms on Trends.

### Inferences
**How this changes the prior desk research:**
- **NCA espresso (45% past-week in 2026 vs 40% in 2022):** Consistent. "Espresso machine" search is up in level from 2022 to 2025 and still growing YoY in late 2026. Breville and new budget machines (Casabrews, Chefman, Ninja Luxe) dominate rising queries, which supports a growing pool of 51/54mm owners. But accessory search volume is very small relative to machine search, and the YoY growth in accessory terms has largely stalled outside the December gifting window. The accessory opportunity is narrower and more seasonal than the NCA top line suggests. Launch timing needs to hit Nov–Dec.
- **APPA cat households +5%:** Search interest for cat water fountains is growing much faster than cat household counts. That points to rising product adoption per household, not just population growth, and supports the fountain + filter consumable thesis. The filter-replacement share of Amazon best sellers and the rising "catit pixi water fountain filter" query support a recurring-revenue angle.
- **Spate / third-party trackers:** I could not cross-check them directly. My Trends data reveals a spring-2026 platform-wide anomaly, so any third-party tracker built on Google data that reports large 2026 YoY spikes (especially Mar–Jun 2026) should be treated with caution.
- **Competitive density:** Ad-library density is unknown (Meta and TikTok were inaccessible). On Amazon, cat fountains are larger and more crowded. Espresso accessories are smaller, with lower review moats, but have a strong brand incumbent (Normcore shows up as "Breakout" across all accessory queries) and cheap generics.
- **Petlibro risk signal:** Petlibro's Aug 2026 search spike coincides with "petlibro outage", "app down" and "feeder offline" rising queries. That is a possible reliability or brand-trust opening for competitors in smart feeders and fountains. This is an inference and was not verified with news sources.
