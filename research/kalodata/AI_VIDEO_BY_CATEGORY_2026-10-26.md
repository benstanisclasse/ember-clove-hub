# Where AI videos do best, by category (2026-10-26)

Data: core Kalodata download. **Kalodata only flags AI videos from June 2026** (every earlier month shows 0%), so
this uses June–September 2026 only. The AI flag is Kalodata's own classification.

## View 1: how often AI videos reach a category's monthly top 300 (Jun–Sep, 4,800 videos per category)
Overall 4.7% of top-300 videos are AI, rising from 4.0% in June to 5.8% in September. Once there, AI videos sell
about the same as human ones (median 0.8–1.1×) and more per 1,000 views ($20.4 vs $15.7), but most are paid ads
(70–95% in most categories).

## Takeaways
- **Best fits for AI video:** Books, Menswear, Tools & Hardware, Health, Toys, Home Improvement; also Muslim
  Fashion, Kitchenware, Home Supplies and Furniture by top-list presence.
- **Weak fits:** Womenswear, Shoes, appliances, electronics, luggage. In Womenswear AI videos rarely reach the top
  (3.8%) and none of 61 in the September sample made $1K. When one does reach the top, it sells like a human video.
- AI's share of top videos is growing every month.
- For us: our P01/P02 try-ons are in the weakest kind of category for AI. Menswear is the fashion category where AI
  does best.

## Limits
Four months of AI data; Kalodata's AI flag is its own estimate; view 2 covers about 4 products per category.
