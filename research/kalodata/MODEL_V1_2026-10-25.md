# Product model v1: predicting which TikTok Shop products keep selling (2026-10-25)

Data: the core Kalodata download, US, 28 categories (Virtual Products has no data), Oct 2025 – Sep 2026.
230,052 product-months, 115,702 products. Code: `scripts/train_product_model.py`. Outputs:
`research/kalodata/model_v1_2026-10-25/` (results.json, models.joblib, panel.csv.gz, September scores).

**How it was tested:** trained on earlier months, scored on the last three months it never saw (forward in time),
the way it would be used. Results held steady month to month.

## Results
| Question | Test months | Base rate | Model AUC | Growth alone | Model's top 10% | Top 10 per category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Holders, 1 month:** a top-500 product is still top 500 and selling at least as much next month | Jun–Aug | 30% | **0.69** | 0.65 | 50% | 57% |
| **Holders, 3 months:** still in the top 500 three months later | Apr–Jun | 47% | **0.80** | 0.53 | 91% | 94% |
| **Breakouts, 1 month:** a rising product outside the top 500 enters it next month | Jun–Aug | 3% | **0.89** | 0.51 | 18% | 24% |
| **Breakouts, 3 months:** enters the top 500 within three months | Apr–Jun | 5% | **0.88** | 0.52 | 27% | 33% |

AUC: 0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect. "Top 10%": share of the model's highest-scored tenth that came true.

- **Breakouts are the most useful result for us:** from risers outside the top 500, the model's top picks enter the
  top 500 at **6× the base rate** (18% vs 3% in a month, 27% vs 5% within three).
- **Staying power is very predictable:** 94% of the top 10 per category stay in the top 500 three months later, but
  these are mostly big, established products (rank, age, months already on the list drive it).
- **Next-month growth is the hardest call** (AUC 0.69, like the pilot's 0.70). The model beats momentum alone.

## What drives each prediction (permutation importance on the test months)
- Holders, 1 month: growth vs the previous month (by far), units, share of sales from the shop page, change vs our
  previous month, seller's share of the category, video share.
- Holders, 3 months: rank, product age, growth, months already in the lists.
- Breakouts: current sales (how close to the top-500 floor), growth, position in the riser list, price vs the
  category median, video share.

## Do creator and video counts help?
Tested on 649 sampled product-months: AUC 0.677 without, 0.690 with. Creators alone 0.56, videos alone 0.56.
A small gain on a small sample: not worth buying many more product details for this question yet. Worth retesting
for breakouts specifically.

## Scores for October (from September data)
- `scores_holders_2026-09.csv`: 13,984 top-500 products with the chance of holding 1 and 3 months.
- `scores_breakouts_2026-09.csv`: 5,188 rising products outside the top 500 with the chance of breaking in.

## Limits
- "Winning" is measured inside Kalodata's lists (top 500 and risers), and all sales figures are Kalodata estimates.
- One year of data: each calendar month appears once, so seasonality isn't learned yet (Nov–Dec holiday not tested).
- The model predicts the market, not our results. Our own products and videos still need testing.
