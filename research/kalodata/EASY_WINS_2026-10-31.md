# Easy wins: 10 products that beat the cable-knit dress on paper (2026-10-31)

Owner asked for an "easy win": 10 products with more potential than the current test product. This is read-only research.
No Shopify, TikTok, CJ cart, order or listing was touched. No Kalodata credits were spent: everything comes from the local
`kalodata.db`. CJ was used only for free searches, variant lookups and freight quotes. Rows are also in
`EASY_WINS_2026-10-31.csv`.

## Method (Dropshipping Masterplan rules, applied strictly)

1. **Kalodata screen.** Started from the Jul-Sep 2026 US top and riser lists, filtered by product name (the category ids are
   unreliable), in Womenswear, Shoes, Fashion Accessories, Jewelry, Bags, Textiles and Sports. Kept items with Sep revenue of
   $35k or more and 70%+ video revenue. Dropped brands and dupes (Comfrt, Halara, Cider, Kitsch, PAVOI, YEOREO, Crocs, Skechers
   and others), intimates and shapewear, beauty, supplements, electronics, men's and kids' items, wigs and jeans. Also dropped
   types already tested (barbed wire necklace, suede clog, cable knit midi, long coat, chunky cardigan, knee boots, pleat mini).
   Nov-Dec 2025 lists were used as a check on Q4 behaviour.
2. **33 product types** went to CJ. Search used generic keywords. The pick was a common variant (size M or a common colour),
   priced with `calculate_freight` to the US for 1 unit, using the cheapest method that arrives in 12 days or less. That was
   LuWei Ordinary US (5-11 days) every time.
3. **Economics.**
   - Delivered cost = CJ variant price + freight.
   - Course price comes from the table: delivered $4-7 → $24.95, $8-11 → $29.95, $12-14 → $34.95, $14-15 → $39.95,
     $15-18 → $44.95, $18-20 → $54.95.
   - Market price = Kalodata Sep unit price of the reference TikTok product.
   - **Suggested price = the lower of the two.**
   - Kept per order = price − delivered cost − 2.9% − $0.30 − 5% refunds.
   - Breakeven ROAS = price ÷ kept.
4. **Gates.** Breakeven ROAS must be below 2.36 (the dress) and delivered cost at most $20. There must be a real CJ listing, and
   sizing must stay within S-XL or one size. Strictly applied, that last rule also rules out shoes with numeric sizes.
5. **Score (0-100).** Each factor is rated 1-5:

   | Factor | Weight | Based on |
   |---|---|---|
   | Demand | 25 | Sep revenue and the Jul-Aug-Sep trend |
   | Margin | 20 | Linear: ROAS 1.60 = 5, ROAS 2.36 = 1 |
   | Instant visual benefit | 15 | |
   | Q4 timing | 15 | |
   | Fit for women 35+ | 15 | |
   | One size / low returns | 10 | |

   The sum is ÷5. There is a −10 penalty for failing a course must-have outright. Only the sunglasses get it: you can buy them
   at Target, Walmart or any drugstore.

**Read the matches carefully.** None of the CJ photos were compared side by side with the TikTok photos in this pass. Every
match is by title only, except the collared jacket, which was photo-checked on 10-30 ("partial: CJ is corduroy"). Most picks
are new CJ listings with few stores and unverified stock.

## The picks

**1. Birthstone Celtic knot necklace** (score 80.4)
- **2-second hook:** a close-up of a hand opening a small box under warm light. The pendant catches the light, with on-screen
  text "her birth month, in a knot that never ends".
- **Risks:**
  - CJ's piece is a witch's knot with a birthstone, not a true triquetra, so the photo must be checked.
  - It is plated alloy, so tarnish could drive refunds.
  - Only 10 CJ stores list it.
  - There is just one month of demand data.
  - Kept is $9.43 per order.
  - No claims about meaning or "protection" beyond the design itself.
- **Boxes ticked (5):** expresses love/gift, extremely unique, women-dominated, improves confidence, saves money versus jeweler
  prices.
