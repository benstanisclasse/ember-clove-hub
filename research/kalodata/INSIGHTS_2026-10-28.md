# Kalodata insights for Ember & Clove (2026-10-28)

Owner: "i would like you to look at the kalodata data we download that is what i would like you to look at and tell me
insights that you are able to find". Read-only analysis of `research/kalodata/kalodata.db` (no new credits).

Data: TikTok **Shop** US, top 500 products per category per month and the top videos per category per month,
Nov 2025 to Sep 2026 (Oct 2025 left out: only about a week of data). Revenue is Kalodata's estimate. TikTok Shop
sells in-app, often from US warehouses, so treat this as where demand and prices are, not as our expected results.
One year of seasonality only. Aug-Sep 2026 category totals are about double earlier months while the median top-500
product didn't change, so compare shares, not raw totals, across that break. Product types come from keywords in
product names (approximate).

## 1. November-December changes what sells (Womenswear, top 500)
Monthly revenue in Nov-Dec 2025 compared with the Jan-Jul 2026 average for the same product type:

| Type | Nov-Dec vs rest of year | Share of category, Nov / Jan-Jul |
|---|---|---|
| Leggings / tights (fleece-lined) | **8.4×** | 7.4% / 1.0% |
| Coats / jackets | **3.9×** | 7.6% / 2.5% |
| Sweaters / knit tops | 2.5× | 5.8% / 2.3% |
| Cardigans | 2.1× | 1.3% / 0.5% |
| Hoodies / sweatshirts | 2.0× | 6.1% / 4.3% |
| Jeans / pants | 1.5× | 20.2% / 15.8% |
| Dresses | **0.48×** | 4.5% / 10.5% |
| Tops / tees / blouses | 0.42× | 2.4% / 6.0% |
| Skirts | 0.8× | 1.1% / 1.0% |

- **Boots** are 40% of all Shoes revenue in Nov-Dec 2025 (about $13M a month), against 6-9% in summer, and were
  already back to 28% in Sep 2026.
- **Dresses in winter:** mini, maxi and midi dresses almost disappear in Nov-Dec; the dress styles that hold up are
  sweater/knit dresses (peak in Nov, $1.1M, and again in Sep 2026, $1.35M) and party/satin/velvet dresses (the
  biggest dress style all year, though it does not peak at the holidays on TikTok Shop).
- **Newness matters most at the holidays:** products launched in the previous 90 days made 19% of Nov and 17% of
  Dec revenue, against 9-11% in spring and summer.

## 2. Price: our products sit 2-3× above where the volume is
Womenswear revenue by unit price (Nov 2025-Sep 2026): under $20 22%, $20-30 27%, $30-45 34%, **$45-70 13%, $70+ 4%**.
Above $70, lives (32%) and the shop page (16%) matter much more; below $70 videos bring 82-89% of revenue.

Coats are the one type where higher prices are normal: 33% of coat revenue is at $45+.

| Our product (price) | What the top sellers charged, Nov-Dec 2025 |
|---|---|
| Two-Button Notch Lapel Long Coat ($100.04) | long wool-look coats: median $45; best sellers $33-59 (camel/blue double-breasted mid-long coats at $45-49 sold ~10-13K units each); 6% of revenue above $70 |
| Snap Button Fold-Over Knee Boots ($91.16) | knee/tall boots: median $49; 51% of revenue at $45-70, 11% at $70+; best sellers $43-58 |
| Stand Collar Chunky Knit Cardigan ($62.56) | cardigans: median $30, **none** of the top ones above $45 (top seller an oversized open cardigan at $29.62, 31.6K units) |
| Cable Knit Mock Neck Midi Dress ($52.99) | sweater/knit dresses: median **$18** (top seller a mock-neck knit dress at $17.67, 25.9K units) |
| Mock Neck Side-Pleat Mini Dress ($170.16) | far above any top-selling dress band |

For our own site with 2-3 week delivery, price is probably the biggest thing working against conversion; the
closest match to TikTok Shop's price bands is the coat and the boots.

## 3. What the top-selling videos look like (Womenswear, 3,300 video-months)
- **Paid boosting is the norm:** 90% of top videos ran as ads and they made 91% of top-video revenue. Median ad ROAS
  2.89 (middle half 2.57-3.20) with in-app checkout.
- **Benchmark: about $22 of sales per 1,000 views** (median, boosted and organic alike). Our TikTok test reached
  about 7,100 impressions on 10-28; at that rate a TikTok Shop seller would have expected roughly $150 in sales.
- **Views matter, likes don't:** revenue tracks views (rank correlation 0.43) much more than likes, comments or shares
  (0.18-0.23). Don't judge a sales video by its likes.
- **Fresh creative:** a top video is typically 36 days old at month end and 42% are under 30 days old.
- **Spread out, creator-led:** 1,221 creators; the top 10% of videos take only 23% of the revenue; 349 creators had 3+
  top videos (affiliate creators with samples, not one viral video).
- **AI video is rare among top womenswear videos:** about 1% (rising from 0% to 5% between Jun and Sep 2026), with
  slightly lower median revenue than non-AI. (The 25% "AI" figure in the 10-25 findings came from keyword searches
  for knit/turtleneck dresses, not from the top videos.)
- Titles of videos with the most sales per view lean to **jeans, pants, denim, hoodies, "fall outfits", stretchy,
  casual** (Halara is the standout brand); bras and dresses are over-represented among low sales-per-view videos.

## 4. What is rising right now (Sep 2026 risers, Womenswear)
Of 998 fast risers' revenue: jeans/pants 28%, sweaters/cardigans 16%, coats/jackets 12%, dresses 8%, hoodies 4%.
Examples: sculpting straight-leg jeans ($60.51), trouser jeans ($67), a sherpa wearable blanket poncho ($38),
a velvet lace blouse ($33), fleece-lined sweatpants ($25), a loose double-breasted long coat ($38), a sherpa
hooded coat ($24).

## 5. What this suggests for Ember & Clove (to decide, not done)
1. **Lead the holiday push with coats and knee boots**, the two products whose prices are closest to where buyers
   spend, and the two types that grow most into Nov-Dec. Keep the mini dress out of paid ads.
2. **Fleece-lined tights** are the biggest seasonal mover in womenswear (8.4×, $15M in Nov-Dec 2025 at ~$15-21)
   and pair with every dress and skirt we sell: worth sourcing as a low-price add-on.
3. **Re-check prices** on the cardigan and the cable-knit dress against $30-45, or position them as premium with
   proof (fabric, fit facts) in the ads.
4. **Judge ads by views and sales per 1,000 views (~$22 benchmark), not likes**, and plan new creative every few
   weeks.
5. **Jeans/pants** are the largest and fastest-rising womenswear type this fall; we have a few jeans styles worth
   testing after the rebate trial.
