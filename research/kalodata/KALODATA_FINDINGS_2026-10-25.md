# Kalodata research: TikTok Shop US, women's dresses & knitwear (2026-10-25)

Source: Kalodata Open API, US, last 30 days (pulled 2026-10-25). Script: `scripts/kalodata.py`. Raw responses:
`research/kalodata/raw/2026-10-25/`. Tables: `categories_womens.csv`, `products.csv` (688 rows / 483 products),
`videos.csv` (500), `product_details.csv`, `comment_insights*.csv`.
Cost: **10.4 credits ≈ $1.04** (4.6 left).

**Read with care:** this is TikTok **Shop** data (in-app checkout, often US-warehouse delivery, affiliate creators).
We sell through ads to our own site with 8–15 day shipping, so use it to compare options, not to predict our CPA.
Revenue and ROAS are Kalodata estimates. Keyword search is loose (e.g. "sweater dress" also returns coats and sweaters),
so every table below is filtered by product name.

- **Video is the channel.** 85–100% of top dress revenue comes from videos, not lives.
- **Our break-even ROAS is ≈ 2.0.** The *median* ad in this niche only breaks even for us, even with in-app checkout.
  To profit we need top-quarter ads (ROAS ≈ 2.2–3.8+).
- **AI videos are normal here.** About a quarter of knit/turtleneck dress videos are flagged AI, and an AI formal-dress
  account (@dress.diary5) made $22K at ROAS 2.04 from one video.
- **Hooks behind the top sellers:** "the most flattering sweater dress", "the sweater dress of my dreams bc it's actually
  flattering", "I'm 5'9" – 140 wearing the medium", "Autumn knit dress season is officially here", "this silhouette is 👌".
  The common thread is **fit and flattery, shown on the body with concrete size information**.

| CurvySweet knitted maxi ($58–67) | Length, sizing inconsistency, itchy/heavy fabric, returns | Flattering on many body types, soft stretchy fabric, weddings/formal |

Two risks stand out for us:
- **Size and length.** P01 now has a size chart; keep pointing people to it.
- **"Doesn't look like the ad."** Our ads are AI-generated from supplier photos, and nobody has seen the real garment.

## 5. What this means for Ember & Clove
1. **P01 is still the better of the two.** Fitted, flattering knit dresses are what sell at $45–65. Lean into the
   *covered but fitted* angle with facts (fitted through the hip, mock neck, midi length, size chart). That angle
   (A3 in the ICP) has no ad yet. Avoid "most flattering" style superlatives.
2. **P02 is commoditised.** Look-alikes sell for $28–34 on TikTok Shop, and the closest one's top complaint is "not like
   the photos". Keep it in the test with the strict stop rule (pause above ≈ $27 per sale).
3. **Order one sample of each dress before scaling** (EPROLO cost + shipping: P01 $26.02, P02 $22.12). It checks colour, length, fabric and
   size against the chart and the AI videos, and gives you real photos and video for later ads.
4. **Next-product directions the data supports** (check weight and cost in EPROLO before choosing):
   - **Dressy knit maxi/midi dresses for occasions** (weddings, holiday parties): proven at $50–65.
     Heavy, so check shipping.
   - **Knit two-piece sets:** Suits & Sets is +20%, and a cable-knit 2-piece set video made $9.4K at ROAS 2.25.
   - **Cable-knit cardigans:** the top "cable knit" video ($10.2K, ROAS 2.12) was a long cardigan.
   - **Knit loungewear sets:** the category is +22% and fits the P02 "comfortable but dressed" buyer.
5. **Make new ads around fit and flattery shown on the body,** with honest size details. That's the most consistent
   pattern among top sellers.
