Sources: monthly top-500 products per category (core download, Nov 2025 – Sep 2026; Oct 2025 is incomplete) and
Kalodata subcategory rankings for the last 30, 90 and 365 days (0.9 credits). Subcategory history by month isn't
available from Kalodata's API (category rankings only take rolling windows), so monthly detail below category level
comes from grouping product names by type (approximate).

## What this means now (late October)
- **Into November:** boots and booties, slippers, leggings, women's pants, hoodies and sweatshirts (both sexes),
  coats, and gifts (toys, electronics, kitchen, party supplies, fashion watches, jewelry) peak. Our P01/P02
  sweater dresses fall in a type that peaks in spring (dresses, May); sweaters and cardigans peak in September and
  stay strong in Nov–Dec.
- **Plan for spring:** dresses, sets, tops and sandals rise from February and peak in May–June.
- **Steady all year:** shapewear, beauty, health. Good for products that don't depend on a season.
