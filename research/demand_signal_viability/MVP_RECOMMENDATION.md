# MVP recommendation (2026-11-01)

## Recommendation: don't build the platform now

The original proposition, "discover emerging demand before it becomes obvious", is not supported (Stage B), and the
product is legally blocked as designed (Stage D). Building software now would mean building ahead of three unknowns:
- permission;
- proof of the precision edge;
- any customer demand.

## What to do instead, in order

| Step | What | Cost | Unblocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Run the forward validation** (`PROSPECTIVE_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`), starting with the approved October sealed pull on 2026-11-02 | ~$9/month in credits | Gates V1-V5 |
| 2 | **Send the licensing questions** to Kalodata and CJ (drafted in `_work/stage_d_licensing.md`), including whether a **customer-supplied API key** model is allowed | Free (owner approval to send) | Gate D |
| 3 | **Willingness-to-pay test**: a one-page offer ("weekly research-triage brief with a public track record"), ~10 interviews with experienced dropshippers, a waitlist or pre-order button | ~$200-300 of ads (owner approval) | Gate E |
| 4 | **Only if steps 1-3 pass**: build the smallest product below | Engineering time | — |

## The smallest coherent product (if gates pass)

**Product:** a **weekly research-triage brief** for experienced dropshippers and small research teams.

**Positioning:**

> "Fewer dead ends: products just below the TikTok Shop bestseller line that are likely to break in, filtered for
> brand traps and margin, with every past call published."

**Core features** (chosen from the Stage D scoring):
1. **Evidence log and public scorecard:** every flag timestamped, with its outcome a month later, misses included. Score 21/21.
2. **SKU-level break-even calculator** with the formula shown: CJ variant cost, freight to the buyer's country, fees,
   returns, a duty scenario, and break-even CAC and ROAS. Score 19.
3. **Signal explanation:**
   - why it was flagged;
   - the calibrated probability;
   - whether it's **returning or new**;
   - category newcomer survival. Score 19.
4. **Break-in list** (ranks 501-1,000, 20 per category). Score 15.
5. **Supplier match with confidence:** close substitute, substitute, or brand design; "photo-verified" only after a
   photo check. Score 13.

**Deliberately left out of the MVP:**

| Feature | Why left out |
|---|---|
| Alerts | The lead-time case isn't shown |
| Growth-stage classification | Not validated |
| Watchlists | Per-user API cost; licensing |
| Personalisation | No data |
| "Winning product" claims | Contradicted by the research |
| AI-video tools | Out of scope |

**Delivery:**
1. **First:** hand-assembled for ~10 users ("concierge"), only if licensing allows sharing outputs. Otherwise, as a
   tool that runs on each customer's own Kalodata and CJ keys.
2. **Software:** only after 3 months of scorecard data and at least 5 paying users.

**Pricing hypothesis, to test, not assume:** $29-49 a month. That sits in the band of comparable research tools and
above our cost floor ($12-26 for a 70% gross margin).

## Claims the product must never make

- "Finds winning products" or "guaranteed to sell".
- "Before anyone else" or "before it goes viral". The research shows no such lead.
- "Market-wide coverage". We see ranks 1-1,000 and risers only.
- "Verified supplier" without a photo check and a sample.
- Any profit figure without its stated assumptions: duties, returns, ad cost.
