  released in weekly drops (e.g. 149 in the week to 10-02, 107 and 94 in late August).

  250 current New Arrivals**. Quartiles $23 / $68.

  halter tops 12, midi skirts 11.

## Third-party estimates (not audited; methods differ)
- Revenue: ECDB, about US$22M for 2025 (flagship domain); Grips, US$25.3M online sales for 2024, +50% on the year before.
- Traffic (Semrush, peppermayo.com, all regions): about 3.4M visits in Oct 2025, 2.94M in Sep 2025, 3.59M in Aug 2025;
  a Jan 2026 table shows about 2.23M. Traffic is 56% direct, 23% Google.
- Rough orders: US$22–25M a year at a US$60–80 order value is about 250k–400k orders a year, roughly 700–1,100 a day.
  That's arithmetic on estimates, not a measured figure.
