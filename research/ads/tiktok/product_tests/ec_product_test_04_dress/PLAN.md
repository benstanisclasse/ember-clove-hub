### Why A is the buyer-side choice
- Nobody pays more than today: one dress costs $2.05 less in total, two cost $16 less.
- The $5.95 shipping fee is under our real $15.05, so we make nothing on shipping.
- No made-up "was" price (no compare-at), no countdown timers, no "only X left".
- B is cheaper for the buyer but leaves $17.83 per single order, break-even ROAS 2.69: too thin to test with ads.
- Honest caveat: the course says break-even ROAS above 2.0 makes profit hard. Every option here is above 2 because
  shipping is $15. A cheaper-to-ship dress (lighter, or a US warehouse) would fix this better than any price.
