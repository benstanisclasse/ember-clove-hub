# Creative research briefs — test 01 (Phase A, 2026-10-28)

Read-only review of research/kalodata (incl. kalodata.db), research/meta, research/tiktok, reports/ and data/catalog.
All findings are observational: hypotheses, not rules. No ICP report exists for these 10 products (only P01/P02).
Most research is about video; image transfer is noted where it matters. Evidence: strong / moderate / weak / none.

## P001 Mock Neck Side-Pleat Mini Dress ($170.16; Brown/White; polyester; sleeveless)
- Motivations: occasion (holiday party), visual attraction, differentiation (sculpted hip pleats from metal rings).
- Appeal: sleek, architectural mini that reads dressed-up yet minimal.
- Objections: price (top-500 pleated/mock-neck analogs $23–35); sleeveless/White off-season; structured-mini fit; unknown store.
- Patterns: on-body first card; ring-and-pleat close-up; styled with tights/boots/blazer to make it seasonal.
- Positioning: 4 loose analogs ever in the top 500 (median $34); premium exists only for brands (SKIMS knit mock-neck mini $131 riser).
- Angles: (1) holiday party, one piece — Brown with black tights and boots; (2) pleat detail as hero (demo-first);
  (3) winter-proof the mini: dress → tights + boots → coat over it.
- Evidence: weak.

## P003 3D Rose Blazer Mini Dress ($122.32; black suiting; long sleeves; red 3D roses)
- Motivations: differentiation, identity, occasion (office/holiday party), seasonal (long sleeves).
- Appeal: a statement blazer dress that is the whole outfit.
- Objections: price; whether the roses look cheap in person; structured fit; work-appropriate?
- Patterns: rose close-up; full silhouette first; face looking at the roses.
- Positioning: no rose/3D-flower/blazer dress in any Kalodata top-500 or riser list; Festève holiday minis $125–150 on Meta.
- Angles: (1) the office holiday party dress; (2) detail-first: roses → lapel → full look; (3) blazer and dress in one (outfit done).
- Evidence: none–weak. Risk: supplier watermark ("aishang meyzis") in a photo.

## P004 Metal Toe Cap Wedge Mules ($113.68; Ivory/Black/Cognac; faux leather)
- Motivations: visual attraction, outfit completion (cropped jeans/trousers); little seasonal pull.
- Objections: price (heeled/wedge mules top-500 median $34, none ≥ $60); backless in cold weather; sizing (no heel height).
- Positioning: autumn mule demand is suede clogs ($2.9M in Sep 2026); sandals/slides $0.7M/month Nov–Dec vs $11.2M Apr–Jun.
- Angles (low priority): commute closed-toe slip-on; metal-toe macro; the shoe for cropped trousers.
- Evidence: moderate that it is out of season. Risks: handle says "genuine leather"/"high-heeled" (supplier spec: plastic
  upper, low wedge) — say faux leather only; stamped brand on an insole photo. Recommend revisiting Feb–Mar.

## P005 Two-Button Notch Lapel Long Coat ($100.04; Burgundy; wool-look polyester; below knee)
- Motivations: seasonal, practical, outfit completion, visual (color).
- Objections: price (long coats top-500 median $42.50, IQR $34–58); "is it wool?" (no); sizing (top complaint on the leading
  analog, 9 mentions; also "material cheap/stiff" 6, "color not as pictured" 4); one color.
- Patterns: full-length on-body first card (no on-model supplier photos → AI, owner checks realism); layering card over a knit.
- Positioning: strongest seasonal analog set — long coats 18 products/$2.5M in Nov 2025, 20/$2.8M in Dec, 19/$2.1M in Sep 2026;
  leaders $49–55. Buyers praise "oversized for layering" (10 mentions).
- Angles: (1) the coat that goes over everything (versatility); (2) burgundy statement over neutrals; (3) cold-week outing look (no warmth claim).
- Evidence: moderate. Never say "wool" (only "wool-look"). Organic Facebook AI post scheduled 11/05.
