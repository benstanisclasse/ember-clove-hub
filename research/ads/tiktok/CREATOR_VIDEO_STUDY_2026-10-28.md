# Creator video study: sweaters, cardigans, dresses (2026-10-28)

Owner: "Yes, study the best videos from the top creators who sell sweaters, cardigans and dresses, and pull out hooks
and formats we can use in our next TikTok ads."

## Method

- Picked from Kalodata (`research/kalodata/kalodata.db`, US, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026): the highest-revenue videos whose
  caption names a sweater, cardigan, knit or dress (no shapewear, swim, sportswear or "dresser" furniture), at most
  2 per creator. 24 picked, 2 dropped as off-topic (rain jacket, tights), 1 blocked by TikTok: **21 studied**.
- Downloaded from the public TikTok links for analysis only (same route as the 10-26 video pilot), transcribed with
  faster-whisper, cuts measured, frame sheets checked by eye for 6 key videos. Videos deleted afterwards; transcripts
  and numbers kept in `creator_video_study_2026-10-28.json`.
- **Limits:** winners only, no comparison group, so these are patterns to copy and test, not proven causes. Revenue
  is Kalodata's estimate. **20 of 21 were boosted with paid ads**, mostly by the brand.

Silent "reaction" videos (husband reacts, back reveal) got the most views (8-13M) but sold less per view.

## Format that wins: the one-take talking try-on

1. Phone on a tripod at chest height, full body in frame, at home (living room, kitchen, patio, backyard), daylight.
2. Talking from frame 0, no intro. The garment is already on or in her hands, often put on in the first 1-3 s
   (cardigan pulled over a plain tank).
3. A real-looking woman, about 30-45, everyday body, jewelry, relaxed; moves, twirls, turns to show the back.
4. She walks through the details as she talks: fabric feel, sleeves, buttons, pockets, length ("hits mid hip"),
   colors, care ("I hang dry it").
5. **Fit facts:** height, weight, size worn, true to size or not, said out loud or as small text in a corner
   ("5'7, 215 lbs, size 14, wearing XL").
6. Two or three styling ideas ("with leggings and a tee", "front tuck", "with boots and an oversized sweater").
7. Basic outfit underneath (white tank, jeans) so the product is the only thing to look at.

Variations that also sold: trying on several colors in one video (torijflow, 78 s), and replying to a commenter's
question ("Replying to @Missy: not oversized but not fitted").

## Hooks (first 3 seconds), grouped

| Hook type | Real examples (paraphrased) | Our version (no price, no delivery) |
|---|---|---|
| Body callout | "For the girls with a belly like mine..."; "I have an apron belly, you don't see it in this"; "Dream dress for us curvy girls" | "For the girls who want a dress that doesn't cling to the tummy..." |
| Season prediction | "This cardigan is going to be the cardigan of the season"; "the dress you'll be wearing all spring and summer"; "my most worn thing in 2024" | "This is the sweater you'll live in all winter." |
| Who it's for | "This is the Everyone Cardigan: corporate, teacher, lounging at home, college"; "the dress all the girls on the go need" | "Office, school run, dinner out: this one dress does all three." |
| Surprise reveal | "I thought this was just a lounge dress until I saw the BACK"; "I just slipped this on and instantly felt like I have my life together" | "I thought it was a plain knit dress... then I turned around." |
| Touch/feel | "I wish you could feel this through your phone" | "I wish you could feel how soft this knit is." |
| Answer a question | "Replying to @Missy: not oversized but not fitted either" | Reply to a real comment about fit or length. |
| Detail-first | "I saw the sleeves on this sweater and was like, immediately yes" | "The metal rings on this skirt are why I bought it." |

Avoid for us: price hooks ("I saw the price and I'm mad"), which break our no-price rule.

## How to use this in our next TikTok test (after the rebate trial ends 2026-11-04)

1. **Formats to test against our slideshows:** (A) one-take talking try-on with fit facts, (B) silent 10-15 s reveal
   (turn around / detail close-up) with one line of text. Same products, so the format is the only difference.
2. **Hooks to rotate in captions now** (caption text only, no on-screen text): body callout, season prediction, who
   it's for, touch/feel. These fit TikTok's 5-caption rotation we already use.
3. **Where the talking videos come from** (owner decision): real UGC creators with product samples (TikTok Creator
   Marketplace or a UGC platform; rates not checked yet, get quotes; plus sample orders from EPROLO), the owner or a friend
   filming, or an AI presenter. AI is the riskiest choice: the owner judged earlier AI videos as looking AI, and
   winners here look unmistakably real.
4. Always state fit facts. Every talking winner but two did.
